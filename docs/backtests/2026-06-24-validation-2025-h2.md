## Interpretation

This window does not support accepting EXP-005 or EXP-009 yet:

- Baseline produced the highest net profit.
- Baseline had the lowest drawdown.
- EXP-005 and EXP-009 reduced trade count, but did not improve robustness here.
- EXP-009 improved September versus baseline, but worsened July, August, and
  November enough to underperform overall.
- The max lot cap continued to hold at 0.10.

This does not mean the ideas are useless, but it means they are regime-dependent.
They helped the 2026 windows and hurt the 2025 H2 window.

## Decision

Status: `Mixed validation`

Do not accept EXP-005 or EXP-009 yet. The next step should be analysis of why
the session/expiration rules helped 2026 but reduced 2025 H2 performance before
stacking more filters.
